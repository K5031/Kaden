# Agent Note: Duties

Status: rejected — removed: duty mode was redundant beside Continuous, the user's call (2026-11-28); see [no duty mode](../implemented/2026-11-28-no-duty-mode.md)

## Problem

The user's direction (2026-11-10) made Kaden the unit of a fleet of agents that works
without supervision: on a goal until it is reached, or on a duty ("be the marketing team")
indefinitely. A conversation never started work on its own, could not react to the
outside world while other work ran, and nobody could see where its work stood without
reading the transcript.

## Decision

Built, then removed. A person gave a conversation a duty (an instruction, kept up until a
person ended it) or a goal (also ended when the agent called `finish_goal` with a report),
from **☰ → Set a duty…** or headless `--duty`/`--goal`. The agent kept a plan, checked in
on a schedule (the earliest due step, else at least every hour), and paused itself with
`ask_for_help` when only its supervisor could unblock it. Sub-sessions ran their task as a
goal.

The user found duty mode redundant: [Continuous](../implemented/2026-11-23-continuous.md)
covers standing work of the agent's own, and sub-sessions work as Claude Code's subagents
do. What it built that stays (the plan, reminders, waking on the first operation to
finish, a life's schedule) is described in [no duty mode](../implemented/2026-11-28-no-duty-mode.md)
and the Continuous note.

## Alternatives considered

Kept because each is tempting should standing work come back:

- **The KFM drives it,** sending "continue" or the next task. Kaden stays as it is, but
  the judgment of what to do next moves into the fleet manager, which is meant only to
  carry messages.
- **A fixed heartbeat** ("every 30 minutes"). Predictable, but it wakes when nothing
  happened and is late when something did. The duty's maximum interval was this, and a
  life that was woken every minute only said it was still resting.
- **The model may turn standing work on.** Convenient ("keep doing this"), but a vague
  message could start something that spends money indefinitely.
- **A separate mode,** as Continuous Mode was. Retired for good reasons
  ([autonomy into sessions](../implemented/2026-11-07-autonomy-into-sessions.md)).
- **No way for the agent to pause.** Simpler, but a blocked agent on a schedule wakes
  every interval, finds itself still blocked and spends a request each time; that is why
  `ask_for_help` existed, and why a life sleeps until something wakes it instead.

## Consequences

- Standing work is a life's (Continuous) or the user's to ask for again; reminders cover
  "do this later" in any chat.
